About Me

Designing and Developing Digital Solutions that Connect

My name is Choirunnisa Safa Fayzalmi, an Informatics Engineering student at Politeknik Negeri Semarang with a strong interest in application development and UI/UX design. Through academic projects and self-directed learning, I’ve gained solid experience in creating user-centered and responsive digital solutions, including mobile apps, interactive websites, and management systems like attendance tracking tools.

In addition to my design and development skills, I also have hands-on experience in computer networking, particularly in configuring and managing MikroTik devices. I hold an official certification from BNSP (Badan Nasional Sertifikasi Profesi) in the field of networking, which strengthens my technical foundation. I enjoy collaborating in teams and continuously improving my communication and problem-solving abilities. Currently, I am seeking an internship opportunity as a UI/UX Designer, where I can apply my skills to build visually engaging and user-friendly interfaces.
